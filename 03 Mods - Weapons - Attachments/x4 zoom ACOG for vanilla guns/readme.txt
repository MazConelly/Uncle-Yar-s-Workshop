-Module:
x4 zoom ACOG for vanilla guns 

----------------------------------------------------------
-Compatibility:

2D Scope Overhaul: Redundant with the module in this package.

----------------------------------------------------------
-Explanation:

This small tweak will increase the zoom of ACOG scope to x4.

In vanilla, theres a scope inbalance. Dovetail weapons can use four sights, one collimator and three scopes with fixed x4 zoom.

Meanwhile, Picatinni rail weapons can use four sights, but three of them are collimators, and the only scope is the ACOG with a fixed x3.5 zoom. (Five sights, if you count the AC11090 red dot sight, but only the XM8 can use it. Even worse!)

You see the issue, right? Let's ignore telescopic scopes, as sniper rifles already have their own. But Pic rail guns fail behind dovetail ones if you want to use them for long range combat.

Different mods try to solve this, though they do by adding more scopes in one way or another. Both BaS and 2D Scope Overhaul also do this tweak, but in BaS' case it only applies to its weapons, and in 2DSO's case is inconsistent (althought I've fixed that in the module for it in this package).
