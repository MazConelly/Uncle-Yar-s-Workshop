-Module:
Anomalous Mines [Deadmeme]
Original only available in the Anomaly Discord channel:
https://discord.com/channels/456765861953536020/1366094245412343858/1367292504407277720

----------------------------------------------------------
-Compatibility:
Original mod not required.

A patch in Simple IEDs Models can give it models to use.

----------------------------------------------------------
-Explanation:
Isolated item and text files to avoid conflicts.
DLTX conversion of the craft file, and in addition to combining items for the IEDs, you can also craft them as usual.
Parts assigned for dissasembling.
Script changes monkey patched.

Integration into:
Grok's Stash Overhaul

----------------------------------------------------------
-Patches:

Arti's Ballistics
When dissasembling, the new IEDs give 2 units of a corresponding artefact powder.

Banjaji's Crafting System Improvements
The new crafting recipes are adapted to BCSI's changes.

Blast-Objects Overhaul (B.O.O.)
The IEDs will also be throwable as per BOO's features.
This is standalone and BOO is not required.

Remote Controlled Explosives
Avoids crashes to desktop and allows you to plant anomalous IEDs as per Remote Controlled Explosives's features, even in someone's pockets.
