Includes the following:
- Completed full sets of mags for AUG, FAMAS, G36, and SIG rifles, with fast, extended, and casket magazines for each. The load_delay from standard mags is passed to the fast mags and assigned them as the rifles improved magazines.
This means those rifle families are now able to perform in high end content, and you don't have to limit yourself to AKs and STANAG rifles.

- Added a simple 7.62x39 bakelite magazine. Intended for BaS' AK104 Alpha and some other mods, with weapons that would use such a magazine in their models.

- Allow Bizon 9x19 mags, for compatibility with Blackgrowl's Better Bizon (the Bizon mag already has a 9x19 variant in MR).

- BaS Patch. Moved extended and short mags for Kiparis, Skorpion, Sig 220, Remington 700 to base files. Slight ID reworking so all and base mags for the same guns follow the same patterns. Consolidation of weapon assignment files. Disabled redundant/old mags.
This is mostly a reorganizing matter, but if you don't play with BaS, you're able to use some of the mags made for it with both vanilla weapons and other mods.


The Fork + Priler's Interactive 3D PDA 2.0 Enhanced patch will ensure consistent behavior between my fork and Priler's update of the trading feature.
