-Module:
NV+Contrast Upgrades disabler [Mumushkeh]

----------------------------------------------------------
-Compatibility:

Compatibility with mods working on upgrades is untested.

----------------------------------------------------------
-Explanation:

Removes the contrast and NV upgrades from:
	Binoculars
	FN2000
	G3SG1
	L96A1
	M24
	M98B
	SVD (Nimble's variant)
	VSS Vintorez (Nimble's variant)
	VSSK (not with BarryBog's reanimation)

SV98 and TRG are not covered (yet).

Intended to avoid issues that may happen with unexpected interactions between mods working on NV features.
