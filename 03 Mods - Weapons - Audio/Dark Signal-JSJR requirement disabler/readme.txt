-Module:
Dark Signal-JSJR requirement disabler

----------------------------------------------------------
-Compatibility:

----------------------------------------------------------
-Explanation:

This will patch a variety of gun mods that have Dark Signal or JSRS as requirement, in case you don't want to use them (for example, if you find they affect negatively your performance).

The gun will use vanilla sounds, or BaS ones if the mod already requires it. Unlike the other "No BaS x" modules in this package, keeping the BaS assets like the audio files, is trivial without including the configs files to actually have the items in the game.

At the moment it covers mostly JMerc mods, plus Frostychun's EFT Rhino Brothers and Khyber's SNIPS.
