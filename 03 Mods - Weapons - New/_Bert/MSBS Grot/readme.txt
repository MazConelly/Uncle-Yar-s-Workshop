-Explanation:

The tan colored variants have no functional differences to the basic ones, so I made them optional in case you don't want the bloat.
HOWEVER, to make them actually different to the base black 5.56x45 models, these are now the 7.62x39 models, hitting harder and using the AK103 upgrades.
In exchange, the muzzle velocity and fire rate of the 5.56 variants has been tweaked to +100 m/s and 850 RPM.

The mod adds a silencer only used by these weapons, so I made it optional as well in case you don't want the bloat. If you won't use it, by default the weapons will use the NATO and ATG silencers.

Proper inheritances between the weapon variants.

Added short names.

NPC loadouts. 7.62 variants will be a little more rarer thant the 5.56 ones.

Traders. Fixed the assignment to Meeker's files.
Both 7.62 variants are raised one supply tier, and on top the full length variants another one. Work for your integrated tacticoolz.

Icon file made DX8-9 compliant. So much for mocking DX8-9 users in the installer (in this economy?), yet fails completely at a simple canvas size tweak. :V

Tweaked actor effects and Grok's Enhanced Recoil settings. Instead of taking from the RPK, the gun will take from the HK416.

Trade files and Mags Redux patch integrated.

Mags Redux support: the 7.62 variants will have their own magazine, and they will use the AK 7.62 base (there are no STANAG 7.62 mags in MR, so this is the next best thing).

Integration into:
FireModeCheck
Lootboxes
Grok's Stash Overhaul
PDA Interactive
Weapon Parts Overhaul
