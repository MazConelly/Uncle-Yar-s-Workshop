-Module:
Handgun Expansion [Kmack]
Only released in Gamma Discord server.

----------------------------------------------------------
-Compatibility:
NOTE: You still need the original mod. Disable the configs folder. 

Not to use with the modules for Walther PDP and Walther PPQ Q5 Match, those are already included in Handgun Expansion.

If you were already using the mod, a new game is required to apply this module.

----------------------------------------------------------
-Explanation:

Fixed configs trying to use a short name that doesn't exist. And given actual short names.

The FN Hi-Power had its internal ID changed from "wpn_browning_hp" to "wpn_hpsa_hpfp", and  the PPD and Q5 are renamed from "wpn_ppd" and "wpn_ppq5" to "wpn_walther_ppd" and wpn_walter_ppq5".
This way they're better ordered in inventory along the vanilla Browning Hi-Power ("wpn_hpsa") and Walter P99 ("wpn_walther").

"wpn_browning_hp" to "wpn_hpsa_hpfp", to be better ordered in inventory along the vanilla Browning Hi-Power ("wpn_hpsa").


Some of the pistols now will use upgrades closer to their vanilla counterparts:
	- FN Hi-Power: from Beretta to Browning Hi-Power.
	- PDP and PP Q5: from Glock to Walther P99.
	- USP Phantom Mark: from FNP to USP.

NPC loadouts were a bit strangely distributed. Gave it mainly to factions included for new game loadouts, and randomized loadout configurations for veteran and master ranks.

Added the gun to traders.

Actor effects and recoil mods support tweaked, so as to not follow the APS in all cases. All pistols will now follow the weapon that most closely (I'm guessing) fits them.

Mags Redux support. The FN HIgh Power now shares the same files and magazines as the one from MWP, meaning it will use the same propietary mags as in real life.
This will be compatible with MWP along the last update of "Hisalute's Packs - Fixes and Tweaks", but not with MWP alone.
