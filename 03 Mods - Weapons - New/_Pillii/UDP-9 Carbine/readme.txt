-Compatibility:
Note: The original mod is still needed. Disable the gamedata/configs folder.

-Explanation:

NPC loadouts. Reworked. The mod looked up "trainee" and "rookie" sections not existing in the vanilla files.
Novices may carry the weapon in default configuration, while experienced may carry it with any ammo and attachments.
If using the "NPC loadouts files rank fix" module in this package, trainee ranks in between may carry it with any attachment.

Trader distribution. Traders now sell at one supply level lower than before (it is a semiautomatic SMG after all).

Grok's Stash Overhaul
