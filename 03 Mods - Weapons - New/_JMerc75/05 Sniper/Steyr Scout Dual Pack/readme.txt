-Explanation:

Dewankered the description text, given short names, and identified the 7.62 mddel as the Steyr Elite (because of the height-adjustable buttpad on the model).

Scout 5.56 tweaks. Holy power creep Batman:
	-Power and impulse lowered to something more in line with other 5.56 weapons; still stronger than them, but not at the level of a Lapua Magnum rifle.
	-Muzzle velocity of 795 m/s instead of the excessive 1350 m/s.
	-10 rounds magazine instead of 8. This also applies to the Mags Redux support.

Elite 7.62 tweaks. It'll be slightly slower but/and harder punching than the L96 and Walther 2000.
	-Power and impulse same as above, lowered to be in line with other 7.62 snipers, instead of at the level of the M82 Barret.
	-Muzzle velocity of 900 m/s instead of the excessive 1500 m/s.
	-Upgrades changed from SVD to L96.

Traders. Raised the Elite one supply level for all traders.
