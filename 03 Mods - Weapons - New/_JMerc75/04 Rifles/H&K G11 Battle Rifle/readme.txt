-Explanation:

Description has been edited a bit to explain a better what the original G11 was and condense the idea of this redesign.

NPC loadouts. Now masters can carry them, not just veterans. Factions with variants can carry the basic one as well. However, they'll have a reduced chance to spawn with (not so noticeable for the basic, but basically halved for variants).
The Army having it is odd due to the NATO/WP dicotomy, so they're out.

Traders. While the NPC loadouts file distributes the variants to CS, Freedom and mercs, only Nimble sells them. Now those factions do as well, but at a low chance to stock them.

Mercs repeated the G11 Lanze (Long range with Leupold scope) with Freedom. So they now instead get the G11 Dolch (silenced with Aimpro sight). CS still keeps the G11 Schwert (marksman with ACOG)
