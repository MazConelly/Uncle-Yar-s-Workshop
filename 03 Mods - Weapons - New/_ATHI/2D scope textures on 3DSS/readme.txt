-Module:
2D scope textures on 3DSS for ATHI's weapons

----------------------------------------------------------
-Compatibility:

The main files of 3DSS are required (counterintuitive as it sounds).

Additional 2D scope textures are needed for those scopes outside of vanilla and BaS.
For example, Anomaly Tacticool Scopes, or Dots and Reticles Extravaganza.
Fitting textures can also be sourced from MsPizza's P.U.S.S.y.'s Erroneous Reduction for Inconceivably Old Devices.

----------------------------------------------------------
-Explanation:

Weapons prepared for 3DSS will crash an unmodded game. To avoid this crash, even on games running on DX8-9 or without interest on 3DSS, you can install the main files of 3DSS itself. Nothing will actually start using 3DSS itself, but you'll avoid crashing.

However, those weapons also don't use 2D scopes, meaning you can't really aim with them.

And while most of the weapon mods available in Moddb offer 3DSS as optional, ATHI only publishes weapon mods already prepared for 3DSS.

This module gives them those scope textures, except for the scopes ECHO1, T12. and UH2 (a matter of not finding appropiate textures).

However, Gamma's version of 3DSS does something fucky with the red dot sights that causes crashes. Don't @ me, yell at the Gammaers. Loudly.
